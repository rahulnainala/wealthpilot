# Glossary — terms WealthPilot uses

VaR (Value at Risk): the 1-day loss exceeded only 5% of days (95%
confidence), from historical returns.

CVaR (expected shortfall): average loss on those worst 5% of days; always
≥ VaR; better tail measure.

Annualized volatility: daily return standard deviation × √252; equity
~14–20%, debt ~1–3%.

Max drawdown: worst peak-to-trough fall of cumulative value over a window.

HHI / effective holdings: sum of squared weights; 1/HHI = how many
independent equal bets the portfolio behaves like.

Diversification ratio: weighted-average asset volatility ÷ portfolio
volatility; 1.0 = no benefit, >1.3 healthy for mixed books.

Correlation: −1..+1 co-movement; pairs above 0.8 act as one position.

Beta: sensitivity to the market; 1.5 beta falls ~15% when the index falls
10%.

Alpha: return above what beta explains; persistent alpha is rare after
costs.

Sharpe ratio: excess return ÷ volatility; risk-adjusted return.

CAGR: compound annual growth rate for lump sums.

XIRR: money-weighted return handling dated cash flows; correct for SIPs.

SIP: systematic investment plan — fixed periodic buys, rupee-cost
averaging.

NAV: fund per-unit value, computed daily post-close.

Expense ratio: annual fund fee, deducted from NAV daily.

Exit load: redemption fee (commonly 1% within 365 days for equity funds).

Direct plan: no-distributor share class, ~0.5–1% cheaper yearly.

ELSS: 3-year-locked equity fund; 80C benefit under the old regime only.

Arbitrage fund: hedged equity earning futures spread; debt-like return,
equity taxation.

LTCG/STCG (equity): >12 months = long-term, 12.5% above ₹1.25L exemption;
≤12 months = 20%.

STT: securities transaction tax, 0.1% on delivery sells.

GTT: good-till-triggered order — standing price trigger at the broker.

CNC/MIS: delivery vs intraday product types at Zerodha.

T+1: trade settles next business day.

Circuit limit: exchange price band halting a stock/index move.

Demat: electronic share account (CDSL/NSDL).

REIT: listed office-property trust distributing ≥90% cash flows; mixed
taxation.

InvIT: infrastructure yield trust, REIT cousin.

SGB: sovereign gold bond; 2.5% interest, tax-free at 8-year maturity.

Monte Carlo simulation: thousands of random return paths to estimate
outcome probabilities (the engine runs 5–10k paths).

P(success): share of simulated paths reaching the goal target by its date.

p10/p90: pessimistic/optimistic percentile outcomes across paths.

Initial shock: one-time crash applied at t=0 in stress tests, scaled by
each sleeve's volatility.

Glide path: shifting equity → debt/arbitrage as a goal date nears.

Sequence risk: bad returns near withdrawal hurting more than early ones.

Rupee-cost averaging: buying fixed amounts periodically, more units when
cheap.

Rebalancing: restoring target weights by trimming overweights.

Emergency fund: 6 months of expenses in liquid instruments; Phase 0 gate.

Term insurance: pure death cover, 10–15× income.

Super top-up: high health cover above a deductible, cheaply extending base
policies.

Effective holdings, waypoints, ALT/VS/HDG: the cockpit strip reads —
portfolio value (altitude), day P&L (vertical speed), core allocation split
(heading), plus goal ETAs.
